---
term: Agentic commerce
---

Commerce where software agents perform the shopping, not just the paying: discovering merchants, comparing offers, negotiating terms, and completing checkout on a person's or business's behalf. An [agentic payment](/lexicon/agentic-payment/) is the settlement step inside it. The defining shift is that the buyer's attention disappears from the funnel - merchandising, placement, and brand aimed at human eyes give way to structured offers aimed at machine comparison, and the protocols that carry them (see [ACP](/lexicon/acp/), [AP2](/lexicon/ap2/), [x402](/lexicon/x402/)) become the new storefront.
